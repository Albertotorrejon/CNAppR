What can CNAppR do?

analytics

Read counting & GC correction

Precise quantification of genomic reads with advanced bias correction for reliable data.

segment

CBS Segmentation

Circular Binary Segmentation for identifying statistically significant genomic breakpoints.

bar_chart

CNA Scoring

Quantitative assessment of copy number alterations across the entire genome profile.

clinical_notes

Clinical Association

Correlate genomic findings with clinical parameters for translational research insights.

show_chart

R Visualization

Advanced visualization of CNA profiles and genome-wide frequencies using native R plotting utilities.

model_training

Random Forest Prediction

Machine learning integration for automated classification and predictive modeling of clinical outcomes.
